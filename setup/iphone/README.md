# New iPhone Setup

Steps after the standard Apple setup (Quick Start / restore from iCloud or
device transfer). Keep the old phone powered on and nearby until the transfers
below are done.

- Change Phone Name to differentiate from the old phone
  - Settings -> General -> About -> Name (e.g. `gPhone 17`)
- Move Apple Watch to the new phone
  - On the new phone, open the Watch app and pair the watch (it unpairs from
  the old phone and backs up first)
- Setup Camera Settings
  - Settings -> Camera -> Record Video -> `4K at 30 fps`
  - Settings -> Camera -> Formats -> pick the preferred photo and video format
  - Settings -> Camera -> Preserve Settings -> turn on what should persist
- Sign out of ForeFlight on the old phone
  - Do this before the old phone is reset or traded in, so the subscription's
  device slot is released
  - Sign in on the new phone
- Sign out of Garmin Pilot on the old phone
  - Same reason as ForeFlight
  - Sign in on the new phone
- Transfer Signal
  - Install Signal on the new phone, register with the same number, and
  choose Transfer from iPhone when prompted (old phone must be on the same
  Wi-Fi and nearby)
  - Transferring deactivates Signal on the old phone
- Transfer WhatsApp
  - Settings -> Chats -> Transfer Chats to iPhone on the old phone, or use
  the prompt when registering on the new phone
  - Confirm chat history made it before moving on

## Reset the Old Phone (optional)

Only after everything above is confirmed working on the new phone.

- Confirm a recent iCloud backup exists, or that the transfer completed
- Sign out of Apple Account (Settings -> your name -> Sign Out); this turns off
Find My and Activation Lock
- Settings -> General -> Transfer or Reset iPhone -> Erase All Content and
Settings
